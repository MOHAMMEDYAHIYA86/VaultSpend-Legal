Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

VaultSpend ("we", "our", or "the app") is a personal finance tracking application designed to help users record and manage their income, expenses, budgets and related financial activity. This policy explains what information the app may handle and how that information is used.

1. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, VaultSpend may handle:

  • Account information, such as your email address and authentication details.
  • Financial records that you enter, such as income, expenses, transaction descriptions and budgets.
  • Support information, such as support requests, messages and replies.
  • Donation information, such as donation amounts, payment references and receipt information when you use donation features.
  • Security settings, such as PIN and app-lock preferences.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide your account and app features.
  • Display your financial records and calculate summaries.
  • Synchronize supported information with your account.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Review donation submissions and investigate payment references.
  • Protect accounts and maintain app security.

3. Local Storage and Cloud Services

VaultSpend uses device storage for certain app settings and may synchronize supported information with Supabase cloud services. The exact information stored locally or remotely depends on the feature and your app configuration.
