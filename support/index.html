VaultSpend · Help Center

How can we help?

Need assistance with VaultSpend? Find information about your account, transactions, security and app features below.

Account & Login

For login, signup, email verification or password reset problems, first check that you are using the correct account credentials.

Transactions & Budgets

VaultSpend helps you record income and expenses and manage your budget. Check your entered amounts and transaction details if a summary looks incorrect.

Security & PIN

Keep your account credentials and device secure. Use the available app-lock and security settings to protect access to your app.

Donations & Receipts

Submitted donation receipts may require manual review. Uploading a screenshot does not automatically confirm a successful payment.

Account Deletion

To learn how to request account deletion, visit our Account Deletion page.

Contact Support Through the App

VaultSpend includes an in-app Support Center where signed-in users can create support tickets, select a category, describe an issue and view replies.

Open VaultSpend → Settings → Support Center to access the available support features.
