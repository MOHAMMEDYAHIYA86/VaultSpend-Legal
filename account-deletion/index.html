Account Deletion

Last updated: November 14, 2026
You can request deletion of your VaultSpend account using the account deletion option available inside the application.

How to Request Account Deletion

Step 1

Open the VaultSpend application and sign in to your account.

Step 2

Open the Settings section of the application.

Step 3

Find the Account Deletion option.

Step 4

Follow the confirmation instructions displayed by the app.

What Happens After Your Request?

VaultSpend uses an account deletion process that invokes a Supabase Edge Function to delete the authenticated account. The complete handling of related financial records, support information, donation records, uploaded receipt images and backups must be verified separately.

Important: Do not assume that deleting your login account immediately removes every associated record, uploaded file or backup. Some information may remain until the deletion and retention procedures have been verified.

Information That May Be Associated With Your Account

  • Account and authentication information.
  • Financial transactions and budget records.
  • Support tickets and related messages.
  • Donation records and payment references.
  • Uploaded donation receipt images.

If you cannot access your account or have questions about deletion, use the available in-app Support Center.

For help with an account deletion request, contact vaultspend.official@outlook.com.
